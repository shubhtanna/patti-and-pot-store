Our story

Patti & Pot began in 2017 with a single tea counter in a Koregaon Park lane in Pune. We were tired of dusty supermarket tea, so we started buying directly from the small estates whose tea we loved.

Today we work with eleven gardens across Assam, Darjeeling and the Nilgiris. We buy each flush as it comes in, store it in airtight tins and weigh out every order by hand the day it ships.

"Patti" is what we have always called tea leaves at home. The pot is where they become something worth sitting down for.
